BNP Paribas Open Vacation Rental Guide for 2027

By Eann Tuan · May 27, 2026 · 7 min read

The BNP Paribas Open runs for two full weeks each March at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. It draws 450,000-plus over those two weeks, and the accommodation math that works for a three-night weekend trip gets more interesting when you're staying for a full week or longer.

I host guests at three Coachella Valley properties. Two are in Indio, about 20 minutes east of the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. One is in Palm Springs, about 20 minutes west. Here's how I'd think through the options if I were booking for myself.

The Indian Wells Tennis Garden sits in Indian Wells, roughly equidistant between Palm Springs and Indio. Both cities are about 20 minutes by car. The difference is what you come home to.

The Tournament: What You're Actually Signing Up For

Context matters here. The BNP Paribas Open is a combined ATP Masters 1000 and WTA 1000 event, meaning the top players from both tours compete simultaneously over two weeks. It's one of the biggest tennis events in the world outside Grand Slams. Stadium 1 holds just over 16,000 people, the outer courts run early-round matches at ground level, and a grounds pass on a Tuesday will put you closer to a top-10 player than you'd get in the upper deck at the US Open.

Match days run roughly 10am to 10pm. If you're committed, you're spending 6-10 hours at the venue. That's a long time in stadium seats, even in March, and it makes the question of what you come home to more meaningful than it seems at first glance.

Why Indian Wells Hotels Disappoint

Indian Wells itself is a small city. The hotels near the venue are nice, and they price accordingly during tournament week, with rates that routinely hit $400-700 per night at anything decent. The proximity is real. Some properties are walkable or a short rideshare to the garden. But the trade-offs add up.

Hotel pools are shared, which matters more than you'd think after a day in stadium seats under a March sun. Hotel kitchens are absent, which matters when stadium food costs $22 for a sandwich and you're there for a week. Hotel wifi is hotel wifi, which is a problem if you're trying to do any actual work between morning and evening sessions. And for groups, two or three hotel rooms at $500 each is a real number that doesn't work the same way a single vacation rental does.

Stadium 1 packed for a match. Early-round tickets are reasonable. Quarters and semis sell out fast. A grounds pass is underrated for the outer courts, where you can watch practice and early rounds at very close range.

Indio as Your Base

Indio is about 20-25 minutes east of the Indian Wells Tennis Garden, making the drive roughly the same length as coming from Palm Springs. The difference is the rental inventory. Three-bedroom houses with private pools are common in Indio neighborhoods like Indian Palms Country Club, and the rates during BNP Paribas week, while higher than off-peak, don't spike as dramatically as Indian Wells or Palm Springs hotels.

A private pool in March is not a small thing. Desert afternoons in March run mid-70s to low-80s. After 8 hours in stadium seats, coming back to a pool with no time limits, no shared-space etiquette, and no chlorine line is a different recovery than a hotel room shower. If you're staying a full week, that daily return pays dividends you feel by Thursday.

Full kitchen access changes the week financially. Stocking a kitchen on arrival day and cooking breakfast, packing snacks, and making dinner most nights saves real money over seven days of stadium food plus restaurant tabs. The good vacation rentals in Indio have kitchens that actually work, not just a microwave and a two-burner situation.

Wifi, Schedule, and Practical Rental Needs for Tennis Week

A few things that matter specifically for a tournament trip, in rough order of importance:

Fast, reliable wifi. If you're working remotely between sessions, which most people attending a week-long tournament are, bandwidth matters. Ask for a specific speed test result or look for it in reviews. "Good wifi" tells you nothing. 100+ Mbps is useful. Fiber is better. This is the one thing worth asking about directly before you book.

Heated pool. March desert nights drop to the low 50s. An unheated pool in March is an amenity on paper only. Confirm it's heated and ask what the startup process is. Some Indio properties require 24-48 hours of notice to heat the pool. Knowing that on day one saves a disappointing evening on day two.

Proximity to grocery store. You want to stock the kitchen within an hour of arriving. Most Indian Palms rentals are 10-15 minutes from a full grocery. Ask or check the map before you assume. The Stater Bros on Monroe is the closest full-service option from Indian Palms.

Covered outdoor space. March evenings in the desert are among the best weather the Coachella Valley produces. Mid-70s during the day, jacket-comfortable at night, no humidity. A covered patio with real outdoor furniture is where a week in the desert actually lives. It's worth prioritizing in the listing search.

The Palm Springs Option

Palm Springs works for a different traveler. If you want a walkable city, a restaurant scene within walking distance after the last match, and a hotel format rather than a house, Palm Springs earns its place. The drive to the venue is the same length as Indio in normal March traffic. The city is genuinely more interesting for non-tennis hours.

The trade-offs: Palm Springs rates during BNP Paribas week spike sharply, private pools are less common per dollar at comparable price points, and groups of four or more often pay more for less space than they'd get in Indio. For a couple staying a full week who wants city access, Palm Springs makes sense. For a group of six or more, the math shifts.

Tournament Logistics Worth Knowing Before You Arrive

Parking at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden costs $20-40 per day in general lots. The walk from outer lots in March afternoon heat adds up over a week. Rideshare from Indio is straightforward, without the post-match surge being as dramatic as Coachella weekend. A grounds pass plus one or two session tickets to Stadium 1 for quarterfinals or later is often a better two-week plan than buying reserved seats for every session. The outer courts in the first week offer some of the best up-close tennis you'll find anywhere.

The full logistics breakdown, including the Palm Springs vs Indio choice in more detail, is covered in the BNP Paribas Open where to stay guide. It goes deeper on each city's trade-offs and what to look for in a rental specifically for tournament week.

When to Book

The BNP Paribas Open is one of the biggest accommodation drivers in the Coachella Valley, comparable to Coachella weekend for certain Indio and Palm Springs properties. The best vacation rentals in both cities fill for tournament week by November or December of the preceding year. If you attend one year and plan to return, start looking immediately after the current tournament ends. If you're reading this in fall 2026 for March 2027, you're in the right window. By January, inventory in Indian Palms at reasonable rates gets thin.

FAQ: BNP Paribas Open Vacation Rental

How far is Indio from the Indian Wells Tennis Garden?

About 20-25 minutes east on Highway 111. Indian Wells Tennis Garden sits almost exactly between Palm Springs and Indio, making both cities roughly equidistant from the venue in normal March traffic. The difference is what you find when you get there: larger houses with private pools at Indio price points, walkable city access on the Palm Springs side.

Is a private vacation rental better than a hotel for BNP Paribas Open week?

For groups of four or more, usually yes. You get a kitchen (which matters over a week), private outdoor space, no shared pool schedule, and more space per dollar than Indian Wells hotel rates during tournament week. For couples or solo travelers who want to walk to dinner, hotels in Palm Springs make more sense.

What should I look for in a BNP Paribas Open rental?

Heated pool, fast wifi (ask for a specific speed test), covered outdoor space, proximity to a grocery store, and a kitchen with real cooking capacity. A full refrigerator and counter space visible in listing photos tells you more than "fully equipped kitchen" in the description.

When should I book for BNP Paribas Open 2027?

By November 2026 for the best Indian Palms and Palm Springs properties. Tournament week fills some of the valley's most popular rentals before the calendar turns. If you're already planning the trip, check availability now. The Cozy Cactus and Terra Luz take direct bookings at indigopalm.co, which opens availability before Airbnb listings are updated.

Can I bring my dog to a vacation rental near the BNP Paribas Open?

Dog-friendly options exist but book faster than standard rentals during tournament week. Terra Luz in Indian Palms Country Club is pet-friendly (up to 2 dogs, $150 fee), with a private backyard and saltwater pool about 20 minutes from the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. Given that match days run 10am to 10pm, a private outdoor space rather than a shared property makes the arrangement significantly easier for everyone.

Is rideshare a realistic option from Indio to the Indian Wells Tennis Garden?

Yes. From Indian Palms, it's a 20-25 minute rideshare to the venue on the 111, and post-match surges from Indian Wells are less severe than Coachella weekend because the crowd disperses over a longer window. Parking at the venue runs $20-40 per day, so if you're attending multiple sessions, rideshare often comes out cheaper, and it removes the outer-lot walk in afternoon heat.

Do vacation rentals near the BNP Paribas Open require minimum stays during tournament week?

Most hosts in Indian Palms and Palm Springs set 5-7 night minimums during BNP Paribas week, particularly for three-bedroom homes. This is partly because the tournament runs two full weeks, and many guests attend for most of it. If you're only attending a few days, you may have better luck booking a property where the minimum aligns with your stay, or looking at properties where the host takes direct bookings and has flexibility outside platform restrictions. The Cozy Cactus and Terra Luz both accept direct bookings at indigopalm.co.
