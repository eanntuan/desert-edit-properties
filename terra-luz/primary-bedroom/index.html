TERRA LUZ

Primary Bedroom

The room I would most want to sleep in.

The primary bedroom is the one I would most want to sleep in, which is the right answer when you are trying to run a hospitality business. The tile floor came from LA Tile and was installed by Orlin. The total was around $4,200. It is the kind of decision that either makes a room or dates it fast. This one did not date.

The art above the coffee bar area is a large Etsy piece, 190cm, that fills the wall in a way no standard print could. The coffee bar cabinet holds a Keurig in warm stone. The woven lounge chair in the corner came from Facebook Marketplace and looks like it costs three times what it did. The pendant lamps on either side of the bed are from a set Dawn approved after the fourth round of options.

There is a fireplace niche with accent tile that reads as a full design moment on its own. The canopy drape setup did not make it. The rods sat too close to the bed frame to work structurally, so we pulled them before the shoot. Some things look right in a mood board and do not survive contact with three dimensions. The room works completely without them.
