Terra Luz

Indio, California | Earth & Light

Cocooned & Soothing. Vibrant & Rejuvenating.

Old Havana warmth. One Frida-blue pool.

Step through the door at Terra Luz and the pace slows immediately. It's the terracotta courtyard, the Kahlo blue pool catching the afternoon light, the sound of nothing but the desert and maybe whoever's in the kitchen. This is what Old Havana warmth looks like transplanted to the Coachella Valley.

Cuban roasted coffee beans on the counter. Mexican dominos on the shelf. Patterned robes in the closets. The Kitchen Terrace has a pass-through window that slides open toward the pool, so morning coffee becomes poolside conversation without anyone having to move very far. The aesthetic is collected, not staged. Wabi-sabi warmth, terracotta walls, woven rattan. The kind of place that feels lived-in on day one.

Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, sleeps up to six (up to 8 guests). The primary suite faces the pool. The kitchen is stocked properly: real cooking territory. Outdoor dining under the desert sky. Pool is heated.

Why This House

I built Terra Luz around a single idea: most vacation rentals look the same, and none of them feel like anywhere specific. Terra Luz was designed by brand architect Dawn Asher to feel like a place with a history. Old Havana character through a desert lens. If you care about where you stay, this is for you.

The pool catches the light between 4 and 6pm in a way that makes you stop whatever you're doing. The Kahlo blue holds it differently than any other color would. I planned that. I also planned the pass-through window so you can hand someone a coffee without leaving the kitchen. The whole house was designed around moments, not features.

Eann

Location

Indian Palms Country Club, Indio, California. Gated neighborhood with golf, tennis courts, and a community pool on-site.

2.5 miles from Empire Polo Club. Eight minutes by car to Coachella and Stagecoach, no shuttle needed. Twenty minutes to Indian Wells Tennis Garden (BNP Paribas Open). Thirty minutes to Palm Springs. Forty-five minutes to Joshua Tree. Downtown Indio is five minutes away.

Who Stays Here

Friend groups and couples who want a private pool and a space that feels like somewhere. Families who need a real kitchen and a real outdoor room. Design-conscious travelers who want a rental with a point of view. Festival-goers who'd rather walk out the front door than wait for a shuttle. The pool looks exactly as saturated as it does in photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking Terra Luz.

Is Terra Luz pet-friendly? +

Yes. Up to 2 dogs welcome, $100 pet fee. Fenced backyard, dog bowl and treats provided. Pets stay off furniture and may not be left unattended. Email indigopalmco@gmail.com before booking to confirm.

How far is Terra Luz from Coachella and Stagecoach? +

2.5 miles from Empire Polo Club, about 8-10 minutes by car. One of the closest vacation rentals to the festival grounds in the entire Coachella Valley.

What makes Terra Luz unique? +

It's a Latin/Cuban-inspired sanctuary designed by brand architect Dawn Asher. Kahlo blue saltwater pool, terracotta courtyard, wabi-sabi warmth throughout. An aesthetic that feels collected over time rather than staged. The Coachella Valley's only vacation rental with a cohesive Old Havana design identity.

How many guests does Terra Luz sleep? +

Up to 8 guests in 3 bedrooms. Full kitchen, living and dining areas, private saltwater pool, and outdoor entertaining space included.

What is the minimum stay at Terra Luz? +

2 nights minimum. Check-in is 4pm, checkout is 11am. Early check-in or late checkout is available when the schedule allows. Ask in advance.

How much do I save booking directly? +

You skip the platform fees entirely. Airbnb and VRBO charge guests 14-20% on top of the nightly rate. Booking direct means no service fees, no booking charges.
