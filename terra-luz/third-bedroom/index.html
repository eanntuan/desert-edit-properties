TERRA LUZ

Wild Tropics Bedroom

Frida blue walls. Rattan headboard wall to wall. No subtlety intended.

The third bedroom is the most opinionated one, which was the assignment. Frida Kahlo blue paint on every wall, the shade named for her specifically. A rattan headboard that runs wall to wall, built and installed by Orlin. It is not a store piece. Orlin built it here, in the room, and then it lived there.

There is a floating live-edge desk shelf on one wall, and bi-fold closet doors we stained to match the wood tones. A blue striped jute rug anchors the room. Floor cushions in the corner handle the overflow seating without making the space feel cluttered.

There is also a twin trundle under the main bed. That was one of the smarter early decisions: the room works as a suite for couples and flexes to sleep three when a family needs it. We did not want to lose that functionality just because the room has a strong visual identity. Both things can be true at once.
