Where parents finally rest on vacation.

"It's the first time we have truly rested during our vacation with our 3 children." That mom rebooked before checkout. The same feeling shows up in different words across 146 reviews.

Pack-n-play ready in the family amenities closet, waiting when you need it. Sound machines in the kids' rooms. Why I Built It This Way

I got tired of staying places that said "family-friendly" and meant they owned a pack-n-play somewhere in a closet. So I built what I wanted to stay in: every drawer labeled, every kid need anticipated, every logistical headache removed before you arrive. The coffee is ready. The kids' toys are genuinely good. You don't have to plan around the house.

There's a specific moment I think about: 9pm, the kids are finally down, and you're in the hot tub watching the sky go dark over the mountains. That's the moment I built this whole house for. The hot tub earns its reputation after the rest of the night is handled.

Eann

Location

Cozy Cactus sits inside Indian Palms Country Club, a gated community in Indio, CA with walkable access to pickleball, tennis, golf, and a second community pool.

2.5 miles from Empire Polo Club. Eight minutes by car to Coachella and Stagecoach, no shuttle needed. Twenty minutes to Indian Wells Tennis Garden (BNP Paribas Open). Thirty minutes to Palm Springs. Forty-five minutes to Joshua Tree. Downtown Indio is five minutes away.

Who Stays Here

Families with young children who want a rental that was genuinely built for kids, not just technically child-friendly. Multi-family groups splitting bedrooms and sharing the backyard. Festival-goers who need a real home base. If you notice details, you will find them everywhere here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking Cozy Cactus.

How far is Cozy Cactus from Coachella and Stagecoach? +

2.5 miles from Empire Polo Club, about 8-10 minutes by car. You can also walk: 1.5 miles to the festival gate via Eisenhower Drive (~19 min). Golf cart rides within Indian Palms are available from community members during festival weekends.

Is Cozy Cactus good for families with young children? +

It's designed specifically for families. Pack-n-play, sound machines, Stokke high chair, curated toy collection, art supplies, pool safety gate, and a complete labeling system so nothing gets lost. Parents describe it as "the first vacation where I rested."

Does Cozy Cactus have a heated pool and hot tub? +

Yes. Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub with pool lighting, plus a community pool steps away. The hot tub deck has lounge chairs, outdoor dining, a fire pit, and a putting green. Pool heat is available for an additional fee.

Are pets allowed at Cozy Cactus? +

Pets are not permitted at Cozy Cactus. For a pet-friendly option, Terra Luz in Indio welcomes up to 2 dogs ($100 pet fee). Email indigopalmco@gmail.com to confirm before booking.

What is the minimum stay at Cozy Cactus? +

2 nights minimum. Check-in is 4pm, checkout is 10am. Early check-in or late checkout is available when the schedule allows. Ask in advance.

How much do I save booking directly? +

15% versus Airbnb or VRBO. Airbnb charges 15–20% in platform fees. We pass those savings back to you. No service fees, no booking charges.
